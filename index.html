Multi-agent AI · Legal tech · 2026

DigiNyaya

A platform where a citizen files a civil dispute and five coordinated AI agents parse the claim, research precedent, analyse both sides, mediate, and draft a resolution in minutes. Anything weak, out of scope, or contested is routed to a human reviewer instead of the AI.

Began as a hackathon build with Akshat Bagadia, Harshita Shahi and Samarth Chakravarty. The evaluation suite, CI pipeline, safety gate and everything measured below I built afterwards.

  • 78% of 100+ test disputes resolved without escalation, in 2.3 min on average versus about 4 hours manually.
  • RAG over 50+ legal precedents: F1 0.87, hallucination 8% versus a 34% baseline; 54/54 citations grounded on an 18-case golden suite.
  • Root-caused two scoring failures and lifted correct win/lose calls from ~50% to 67%.
  • Hash-chained, tamper-evident audit log; circuit breakers on every AI call; CI gates every PR on the eval suite. Selected for the Sarvam AI Startup Program (2026).

Agentic AI · Fintech · 2026

FinSight AI

An equity-research and paper-trading platform. Ask about any listed company and an LLM planner picks the tools it needs (market data, SEC and NSE filings, DCF valuation, sentiment) and writes a sourced, institutional-style report.

  • Report time cut 4× (257s to 65s); RAG over live SEC EDGAR and NSE India filings with FinBERT sentiment.
  • From-scratch WACC, FCFF and DCF engine with Monte Carlo simulation and a 31-signal factor scorecard.
  • Point-in-time backtest scaled from ~80 to ~1,000 tickers. It showed the rating loses to "always Buy" (38.6% vs 58.4%), so I ship that number on every report.
  • Next.js and FastAPI, provisioned on AWS with Terraform; 1,100+ tests; voice input and PWA.

Graph engine · Backend · BHEL internship

Nexus

Models a company's systems, vendors and supply chains as a directed graph, then answers: if this node fails, what else breaks, and which nodes are single points of failure? The graph algorithms are written from scratch with no graph libraries.

  • BFS, DFS, Dijkstra, max-flow/min-cut, cycle detection, topological sort, Tarjan's SCC, with cascading-failure simulation on top.
  • Fixed an O(E²) bottleneck: 40s to 0.1s on 10K to 50K node graphs (256×), approved by BHEL IT leadership.
  • 94% alignment with domain-expert criticality rankings across 12+ projects.
  • FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy 2.0, PostgreSQL, JWT with role-based access, Alembic migrations, Docker and CI/CD; 141 local tests.

Fraud detection · Graph ML · Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026

Argus

An AI risk manager for promo and referral abuse. Scoring one account at a time can't catch a ring of farmed accounts, so Argus builds a graph of accounts linked by shared devices, payment instruments and referral chains, and flags whole clusters. An LLM only writes the case for a human analyst; no code path bans or blocks anyone.

  • On a frozen, single-pass synthetic holdout: 100% hard-ring recall (40/40), 85% soft-ring recall, 98.7% cluster precision, 2.5% false positives on planted look-alike households.
  • Also checked on real labelled data: 99.2% precision on YelpChi, 7.3× lift on the Elliptic Bitcoin graph.
  • Explicit stage that hunts for evidence a cluster is legitimate, plus a fairness audit against RBI's FREE-AI framework.
